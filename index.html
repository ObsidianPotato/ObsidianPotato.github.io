FruitBrawl is a 1v1 deckbuilder where the shop cards slowly go overripe until they attack both players. FruitBrawl challenges the norms of the deckbuilding genre to create intricate puzzles on every turn.

All of the systems in the game are intertwined, so the complexity extends far beyond what is immediately obvious. FruitBrawl is meant to be replayed over and over again: there are countless strategies and unexpected situations that can occur. A deep understanding of the game will give you the best games (the process to getting there is engaging, though!)

FruitBrawl takes 45-90 minutes to play, if both players are experienced. Add about 30 minutes for each new player to get a more accurate time estimation.
